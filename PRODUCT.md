# RallyDesk — Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

The tournament organizer uses Chrome on a laptop. Other team executives log in on their phones to enter match scores. Players do not need accounts.

## Product Purpose

Keep players, check-in, payments, groups, knockout matches and results together for small table-tennis tournaments. Reuse player records and simple ratings in future events.

## Positioning

A simple shared tournament desk for the varsity team.

## Confirmed Constraints

Usually about 20 entrants, with turnout varying; three tables; a 3–4 hour venue booking; limited money; usually a $10 entry fee paid separately in cash or by card. Likely Vercel hosting. The technology should be minimal, easy to maintain and easy to use.

## Confirmed Scope

Groups → knockout first. Swiss later. Organizer registration and check-in. Payments recorded with a paid checkbox, method, amount and optional discount. Exec accounts can enter scores only. Persistent players and a simple Elo rating are useful across events.

## Principles

- Fewer screens, dependencies and operating steps.
- Fast check-in and score entry.
- Saved results are shared between the laptop and phones.
- Keep tournament rules visible and understandable.

The requirements document records proposed defaults where the user did not specify a rule.
